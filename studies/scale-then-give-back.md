# Scale then give back

Reference-class study with a held-out cohort, 17 September 2026.

## Question

Among companies that tripled in three years, does a particular organisational shape make it more likely they give much of that growth back in the next three?

## How it came about

The configuration was first described from one company, Synthego in 2017: tripled or better over three years at comparable size, at least 8 role buckets, engineering at least 28% of observed staff, operations at most 12%. An earlier screen of the same composition against three-year growth came back null. Rebuilt as a reference forecast with a six-year, two-stage outcome (expand, then fail to hold), it did not.

## Design

- Target state described source-relative; controls matched on momentum first, differing only in composition.
- Outcome: gave back more than half of the three-year expansion over the following three years.
- Development cohorts: base years 2010 to 2011 and 2012 to 2013. Held-out cohort: base 2014, scored with thresholds already frozen.

## Results

| Cohort | Reversal, configuration | Reversal, controls | RR | p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pooled (605 vs 19,502) | 21.1% | 13.2% | 1.60 | |
| Development, base 2010 to 2011 | | | 1.53 | 0.011 |
| Development, base 2012 to 2013 | | | 1.50 | 0.0016 |
| **Held-out, base 2014** | **18.4%** | **7.9%** | **2.33** | **7.4e-06** |

With the outcome widened to giving back 30% or more, the held-out cohort showed 31.4% vs 20.5% (p=0.0006).

## Limits

- The thresholds were drawn to describe one company, so the development cohorts are partly a fit. The held-out cohort carries the claim, and it is a single cohort.
- Operations share on its own is not monotone and does not drive the effect. The signal lives in the joint configuration, which is why univariate screens missed it.
- Use: a caution flag on fast-growing targets, not a reason to buy or avoid anything on its own.

## Lesson kept for every later study

Fix the outcome and horizon before drawing the class. The same configuration is null at three years and significant at six.

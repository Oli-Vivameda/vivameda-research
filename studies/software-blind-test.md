# Blind forward test: does workforce structure rank later winners?

Pre-registered September 2026. Running; no outcome opened at the time of writing.

## Question

Using only workforce and capability structure observed up to 31 December 2020, can we rank which US listed software companies developed best over the following three years, better than a momentum baseline?

## Frozen design

- Specification SHA-256: `53f1e2e70f3552167f45c7a7e3f957bd78a1630ab9d76c39ea45f6738c303d3f`. Frozen and not to be changed.
- Predictor window 2016 to 2019. 2020 is barred as a weighted predictor because of the known method break, and is used only as a consistency check.
- Primary endpoint: Spearman correlation of the primary score with 36-month forward revenue CAGR, plus incremental top-20% lift over momentum. Secondary endpoints stay secondary whatever they show.
- The primary score stays primary. A secondary score cannot be promoted after the fact, even if the primary fails and the secondary succeeds.
- Universe from a point-in-time source including later delistings and acquisitions. No present-day listings, no outcome knowledge used to fill gaps. Acquisition and take-private are outcomes, not exclusions.
- Order: universe, hash, features, scores, hash, then outcomes. Semiconductors is the planned replication with the method unchanged, and counts for more than the software result.
- A separate addendum, with its own hash, tests sector-relative stock returns across all US listed companies: 2017 and 2018 cutoffs first, 2019 and 2020 kept sealed as confirmation.

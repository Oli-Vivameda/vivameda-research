# Exelixis: rebuilding an organization after restructuring
**Owner-approved publication summary. Retrospective reconstruction; historical description, not a forecast.**

## The strategy question
How did the observed organization evolve after restructuring, and what questions should that history bring into diligence?

## Public context
Exelixis disclosed a restructuring implemented in September 2014 after COMET-1 missed its primary endpoint. Its later FY2015 filing reported an aggregate headcount reduction of 143 employees from that restructuring. Public filings therefore already establish the event; Vivameda does not claim to have discovered it.

## What the historical panel adds
In Vivameda's retained three-company analytical test, observed profile counts for Exelixis increased by approximately 219% between 2015 and 2020. That increase describes observations in a retrospectively reconstructed panel. It is not an estimate of payroll employee growth.

The retained analysis also found that classified sales and specialist counts both increased. A falling specialist share alongside an increasing specialist count illustrates why proportions and absolute counts must be interpreted together. Role categories are classifications of observed records, not the company's own departmental reporting.

## What this supports
The observations are consistent with organizational rebuilding and increasing commercial representation. They support a focused research question: how did the company rebuild its commercial infrastructure alongside its scientific and development activity?

That interpretation is conditional. The panel alone does not explain causality, establish clinical success, prove financial productivity or predict investment returns.

## Evidence limits
Panel observations and reported year-end employee totals differ. Population definitions, observation timing and attribution require reconciliation; a count ratio is not verified coverage. Role denominators and provenance overlap remain qualified. Earliest observations are not founding dates. Undated skill records are excluded from this interpretation.

This is a selected descriptive example, not a blinded benchmark or evidence of predictive uplift. No historical reference rate or individual-company probability is supplied.
